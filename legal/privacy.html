3Your voice, specifically

A session is spoken, so the app asks for microphone access. Your voice does more work here than in most apps, so this section is specific about it.

The voice side of a session runs on Hume AI. Your speech is streamed to it live, and it does three things:

  • Converts what you say into text, in near real time. That transcript is what the scribe shows you and what your documents are built from.
  • Reads how you said it. Hume measures vocal expression — tone, pace, emphasis, the qualities of a voice that carry feeling — so that Kaiso can respond to a founder who is hesitating differently from one who is certain. This is the part most people would not expect, which is why it is stated plainly here.
  • Speaks as Kaiso. The voice that answers you is generated by the same service.

What that measurement is, and what it is not. It is a read of how something was said, used in the moment to shape the conversation. It is not a diagnosis, a psychological or medical assessment, or a judgement about you as a person, and nothing in your venture profile, your documents or your readiness grade is derived from it. We do not use your voice for biometric identification, we do not build a voiceprint, and we do not use it to recognise you or anyone else.

  • We do not store recordings of your voice. KaizenOS keeps the transcript, not the audio. The audio passes through Hume to produce the transcript and Kaiso's reply, and we instruct it to keep nothing beyond what that requires. If you want the current position on Hume's own retention, ask us at team@kaizenos.ai and we will tell you what it is.
  • The transcript is kept in your account until you delete it or close your account.
  • The microphone is only active while a session is live. Ending the session stops it, and you can revoke microphone permission at any time in your device settings, though the spoken part of the service will not work without it.

5AI processing

The service works by sending your transcript, and the venture profile you gave us, to large language models that generate Kaiso's replies and your documents. That means:

  • Your content is transmitted to the AI providers listed in section 6 so they can produce a response and return it to us.
  • We instruct those providers not to retain your content beyond what is needed to return that response, and not to use it to train their models. Where a provider offers a zero-retention or no-training configuration, we use it. Anthropic does not train its models on what is sent through its commercial API.
  • Kaiso's replies and the documents it produces are generated by a machine. They can be wrong, incomplete or out of date. The readiness grade the app shows you is an indicative signal, not a valuation, an audit or any form of financial or investment advice. Check anything you intend to rely on.
  • There is no automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects for you.

7Payments

In the iOS app, subscriptions and token purchases are made through Apple's In-App Purchase system. Apple processes the payment and holds your payment details; we never see your card. We receive confirmation that a purchase succeeded, what was bought, and the identifier needed to credit your account.

On the web, payments are processed by Stripe. Stripe holds your card details; we store only the card type, last four digits, expiry, and your billing history so we can show you invoices and handle refunds.

8International transfers

We are established in the United States and the service is hosted there, so wherever you are, your personal data is processed in the United States and in any other country where a provider listed above operates.

If you are in the United Kingdom or the European Economic Area, that is a transfer out of your region. For it we rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and the UK International Data Transfer Addendum, together with additional technical and organisational safeguards where they are needed, and we impose the same terms on the providers we use. You can ask us for a copy of the mechanism we rely on for any particular provider.

9How long we keep it

  • Voice audio — not retained at all.
  • Account, profile, transcripts and documents — for as long as your account is open. You can delete individual documents at any time.
  • After you close your account — deleted or irreversibly anonymised within 90 days, except where we must keep something longer.
  • Billing and tax records — seven years, as our tax law requires.
  • Diagnostics and logs — 90 days.

If you want to complain

Please tell us first, so we can put it right. Beyond that, where you can take it depends on where you are. We are established in the United States, which has no single national data protection authority, so there is no one regulator that oversees us.

  • In the United States — you can complain to the Federal Trade Commission or to your state attorney general. If you are in a state with its own privacy law, that law's regulator applies: in California, the California Privacy Protection Agency.
  • In the United Kingdom or the European Economic Area — you can complain to the data protection authority where you live, work, or where you think the problem happened. You do not have to come to a US regulator.

If you are in California

You have the rights to know, delete and correct your personal information, and to opt out of its “sale” or “sharing”. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined in the CCPA, and we have not done so in the preceding 12 months, including the personal information of anyone under 16. We do not use or disclose sensitive personal information for any purpose other than providing the service. You may use an authorised agent, and we will verify your request before acting on it. We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of these rights.

Residents of other US states with comprehensive privacy laws — among them Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Utah and Texas — have broadly equivalent rights, and the same request address above reaches us.
