It keeps a transcript of every session and a library of what it has produced for you.

4Plans, tokens and time

Talking to Kaiso consumes tokens, which the app shows you as minutes of session time, because minutes are what you are actually spending.

  • The subscription grants a fixed allowance of tokens each billing period, on the date shown in the app.
  • Plan time does not roll over. Whatever is unused at the end of a period expires when the new allowance arrives.
  • Top-ups are one-off token purchases. They do not expire at the end of a period: each top-up stays valid for 12 months from purchase.
  • Sessions draw on your plan allowance first, and on your oldest top-up after that, so nothing expires sooner than it has to.
  • The token cost of a minute of conversation is set out in the app and may change; we will tell you before any change takes effect.
  • If your balance reaches zero mid-session the session pauses. Nothing you have already said is lost.
