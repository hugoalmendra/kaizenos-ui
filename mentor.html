Executive Summary · One-Pager

Helm

The operations autopilot for independent e-commerce brands.

Forged by Kaiso· June 2026· Confidential

The Problem

Independent online stores lose 12+ hours a week stitching together inventory, fulfillment, and customer support across disconnected tools. Hiring an ops manager is expensive and out of reach for the 90% of brands doing under $2M in revenue.

The Solution

Helm is an AI operations layer that connects a store's existing tools and runs the repetitive work — reorder alerts, shipment triage, refund handling, and daily reporting — autonomously, with a human-in-the-loop dashboard.

Business Model

Tiered SaaS from $79–$399 / month based on order volume, plus a usage fee on automated actions. Land at the solo-founder tier, expand as the brand scales.

Why Now

Agentic AI finally makes reliable, low-cost operations automation possible, while a record number of solo founders are launching stores without back-office staff.

Helm is an AI operations layer. Kaiso · Brand Kit
Brand Kit · Starter direction
☰ HELM Primary · on hull
☰ HELM Reversed · on sail

Logo direction

Three stacked bars — a waterline and a helm at once. Reads at 16px in a browser tab, which is where most of a store’s customers will meet it. Wordmark set in the display face at heavy weight, wide tracking, always uppercase. Mark may run alone; the wordmark may not run without clear space equal to the bar height on all sides.

Palette

Hull carries the product chrome, Sail the document surfaces. Tide is the only colour used for a primary action. Signal is reserved for things that need a human — a stuck shipment, a low-stock alert — so it must never appear decoratively. Kelp confirms an automated action completed.

Typography

Display · 40 / 700 Run ops on autopilot
Heading · 20 / 600 Everything the back office does
Body · 16 / 400 / 1.6 Helm connects the tools your store already runs and handles the repetitive work, with you approving rather than doing.
Caption · 11 / 500 Trusted by 1,400 brands

Bricolage Grotesque for display and headings — it has enough character to hold a logo lockup. IBM Plex Sans for body, IBM Plex Mono for order numbers, SKUs and anything a founder will read back over the phone. All three are open-licensed, so nothing here needs a foundry invoice before launch.

Voice

Say
  • Plain operator language — “reorder alert”, not “intelligent replenishment signal”.
  • Name the hours saved before naming the technology.
  • Talk to the founder who is still doing the work tonight.
Avoid
  • “Revolutionary”, “seamless”, “game-changing”.
  • AI as the headline — it is the method, not the promise.
  • Enterprise tone. These are one- and two-person teams.
